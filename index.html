Why does refurbished matter? Buying refurbished skips the mining, smelting, and manufacturing steps, which make up about 80% of an iPhone's environmental cost. The calculator counts a refurbished phone at 20% of a new phone's footprint.

Data from Apple Environmental Reports, USGS, and published life-cycle studies. Carbon figures come from Apple's product reports. Water and raw material figures are estimates. Gold and silver prices are from October 30, 2026.
